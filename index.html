Privacy Policy: Cyber Sentinel Network Monitor

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Cyber Sentinel Network Monitor ("the app") is a network monitoring tool developed by Nikiforos Manolas as part of a diploma thesis at the University of Western Macedonia. This policy explains what the app does with data.

Summary

The app does not collect, store or share personal data with the developer or anyone else. It has no account, no advertising and no analytics.

Network traffic capture (Process Sniffer)

When you start a capture, the app uses Android's VpnService to create a local VPN on your device. Traffic of the app you choose (or of all apps, except Cyber Sentinel itself) passes through this local VPN so that the app can show you its metadata: IP addresses, ports, protocol, packet sizes, and hostnames taken from DNS answers and from the unencrypted Server Name Indication (SNI) field of TLS connections.

  • The traffic is forwarded unchanged to its original destination. It is not sent to any server operated by the developer, and no external VPN server is involved.
  • Encrypted content (for example HTTPS pages) is never decrypted.
  • Captured information is kept only in the device's memory, is never written to storage, and is discarded when the app is closed.
  • While a capture is running, DNS queries of the monitored apps are sent to Google Public DNS (8.8.8.8), whose privacy policy is at https://developers.google.com/speed/public-dns/privacy.

The capture starts only after you approve Android's VPN connection request, and a persistent notification is shown while it runs. You can stop it at any time from the app or the notification.

API Playground

The API Playground sends test requests to the public test service JSONPlaceholder (https://jsonplaceholder.typicode.com). These requests contain only fixed sample data from the app, never your personal data. The results are kept in memory and discarded when the app is closed.

Permissions

  • Internet: to send test requests and to forward captured traffic.
  • VPN service (BIND_VPN_SERVICE): to capture traffic locally, as described above.
  • Foreground service and notifications: to keep the capture running and show its status.
  • Query installed apps: to list launchable apps so you can choose which one to monitor. This list stays on the device.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
